Test Conditions (@ 25°C, no output load)
All delay specifications are measured at 1.5 V on the leading edge with no load on any output tap. Specify Opt.T for trailing edge measurement. Input rise time is 2.0 ns (0.75 V to 2.4 V). Pulse spacing must be ≥2× pulse width. Output tap delays will increase when any capacitive load is present — RCD does not publish a load-vs-delay curve; contact RCD for load-dependent behavior in your application.
